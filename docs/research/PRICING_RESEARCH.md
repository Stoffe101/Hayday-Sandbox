# Hay Day pricing research

Verified: 2026-11-10

Dataset: [`data/prices/hayday_prices.json`](../../data/prices/hayday_prices.json), generated by
[`tools/catalog/build_prices.py`](../../tools/catalog/build_prices.py) from the catalogue (`data/items/*.json`),
[`data/research/items_research.json`](../../data/research/items_research.json) and the hand-checked facts below.
Schema: [`docs/PLANNING.md`](../PLANNING.md) section 1. Validation: `tools/ci/validate_data.py`.

Sources are public fan wikis only: the Hay Day Wiki on Fandom (en, ru and de), read through the MediaWiki API
(`action=query&prop=revisions`, batches of up to 40 titles, 1.5 s apart, generic user agent, about 90 pages).
No Supercell server, game file or image was used. Only facts (numbers, levels, yes/no rules) are recorded; no wiki
prose is copied. Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0, Hay Day Wiki contributors (see each page's history); the price file carries
the same licence and the Supercell Fan Content Policy disclaimer.

## TL;DR

- 1,962 of the 1,967 catalogue items have a price record (the 5 missing are editor-only planning markers).
- Only 447 items can be bought with a known price: 338 in the shop (217 coins, 121 diamonds), 66 with vouchers,
  38 with event currency (37 film, 1 golden keys), plus 5 free. 1,514 cannot be bought (`not_in_shop` 1,500,
  `reward` 14). One item has no known price at all (the Enchanted Workshop).
- The 1,458 "999 diamonds" values are a "not for sale" placeholder and are stored as `not_in_shop` without a price.
- 125 coin decorations get dearer with each copy (`step`). 21 items have an explicit per-copy `schedule` (3
  production buildings and 18 animal homes).
- **The basis of the step is not established.** All three wikis say "per purchase". Stored copies can't be the
  difference: the game won't sell another copy while one is stored. Nobody documents whether deleting a copy, or
  getting one as a reward, changes the next price. The data uses `step_basis: "copies_owned"` with
  `step_confidence` and a top-level `rules.step_basis` entry marked `unverified`.
- Fruit trees, bushes and animal homes cost the same for every copy, according to their wiki pages.
- Expansion supplies have no fixed coin price.

## 1. Price-step rule (question a)

| Finding | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Coin decorations show "N coins +M per purchase" (e.g. Fence 5 +2, Stone Path 25 +5, Rope Fence 1,000 +100). | en `Decorations/Fences & Paths` rev 107633 (2025-03-24) | community |
| Coin decorations get more expensive after every purchase. | ru `Украшения` rev 28189 (2026-05-20) | community |
| Coin decorations have a staggered price that rises the more of them you buy; steps range from 2 to 500 coins per copy. | de `Dekorationen` rev 41351 (2024-11-10) | community |
| A stored decoration goes back to the shop. While one is stored, no more of that type can be bought. | en `Decorations` rev 109109 (2026-01-11), en `Shop` rev 106752, de `Dekorationen` | community (three pages agree) |
| The ru data module gives coin prices as `{base, increment}`. Diamond and voucher prices have no increment. | ru `Модуль:Все украшения` (via items_research.json, rev 2026-09-29) | community |
| Fence step: de `Zaun` (2024-03-17) says the next fence costs 5 coins more each time. The en list and ru module say +2. | de `Zaun` rev 40506 | **unverified** (conflict, recorded on `fence`) |

What this establishes:

- Price for copy *k* = `price + step × (k − 1)`. This is the wiki model, not a measured formula.
- **Placed vs owned:** at the moment of any purchase, the stored count is 0, because you can't buy while a copy is
  stored. So "copies placed" and "copies owned" are the same at purchase time.
- **Owned vs ever bought:** not established. No page says whether deleting a decoration (de wiki: the interaction
  menu has *delete*, with no refund) lowers the next price, or whether reward copies of a shop item raise it.
  Searches of general web results, Reddit mirrors and app-store pages found nothing reliable.
- **Selling:** no source describes selling a decoration back for coins.
- How to resolve: in game, note the shop price of a cheap coin decoration (e.g. Fence), buy one, delete one, and
  check the price again.

The Godot `PriceBook` chooses copy *k* from `profile.purchased[item]` when the user recorded it, otherwise from the
copies owned (PLANNING.md section 1). That works under either basis.

## 4. Fruit trees and bushes (question d)

All eight pages checked say "can be purchased in the shop for N coins each": Apple 160, Cherry 410, Raspberry
220, Blueberry 550, Coffee 375, Banana 800, Nectar 120, Peanut 1,200. The de `Apfelbaum` page also gives 160
coins (or 13 diamonds). No en, ru or de page describes a price that rises with more trees. Trees and bushes are
therefore flat-priced (no `step`). The 13 trees and the 4 bushes found in the ru module are `confirmed`, because
the en and ru wikis agree on every price (ITEM_DATA_RESEARCH.md). Nectar and Peanut Bush have one source and are
`community`. Fields cost 1 coin each (de `Feld`, `community`); their number is limited by level.

## 6. Mapping rules used by the generator

- **Method.** Applied in order:
  1. `removal tool: X` → `not_in_shop` + `removal.tool`
  2. farm buildings → `not_in_shop` (the ru `Магазин` page lists what the shop sells, and farm buildings are not
     on it); a repair cost goes to `repair`
  3. the 999 placeholder → `not_in_shop`
  4. `no price in source (reward/prize item)` → `reward`
  5. `free` or 0 coins → `free`
  6. film or golden keys → `event_currency`
  7. vouchers → `vouchers`
  8. coins or diamonds → `shop`
  9. no cost, with en list group Derby or Rewards → `reward`
  10. no cost, with another known group → `not_in_shop`
  11. anything else → `unknown`
- **Routes** (non-purchase methods only):
  - from the en list group: Derby→derby, Events→event, Packages→package, Secrets→secret, Rewards→reward,
    Expansion→expansion, Pass Road→season_pass; Valley, Calendar, Catalogue and Custom→other
  - from the ru `obtained via` tokens: derby; special-currency, global and task events, county fair and event→event;
    currency-shop offer→package; Farm Pass→season_pass; expansion plot→expansion; collection→collection
  - everything else (valley, Wheel of Fortune, themed box, movie night, design contest, gift, decoration set,
    catalogue, other)→other
  - the raw hints are kept in `notes`. 322 of the 1,514 records that cannot be bought have no route evidence.
- **Availability** (evidence only):
  - `event`: event-currency items, and records whose only route is an event
  - `seasonal`: temporary machines and items on the en "Seasonal" shop tab
  - `current`: production buildings, animal homes, trees, bushes and the field; and decorations that have a real
    price, an ru `obtained via: shop`, no holiday theme and no "monthly decoration" tag, or that are on a permanent
    en shop tab
  - `unknown`: everything else
  - Nothing is marked `retired`, because no source states it.
- **Confidence:**
  - `confirmed`: two separately maintained wikis agree on the price (en list against the ru module, or en infobox
    against the ru tree module)
  - `community`: one community source
  - `unverified`: a recorded conflict
  - `unknown`: no data

## 8. Open questions

1. Step basis (owned vs ever bought; the effect of deleting a copy or getting one as a reward). Check in game.
2. Fence step: +2 (en, ru) or +5 (de, 2024).
3. 21 Sanctuary prices (ru 500 coins lower than en), and the swapped Chinese Money Plant / Water Dragon Steps prices.
4. Whether 999-placeholder items reappear for sale in limited-time offers. The data treats them as not sold.
5. 1,331 records have unknown availability. The wikis rarely say whether an item is still sold.
6. Prices change over time (the 2nd Sugar Mill was 200,000 in one list and is 199,750 now).
